Flight plan

Three decisions, one flight log

Every mission offers three options with different scores and a written consequence. Callsigns, scores and the final rank are held in memory for the session only.

  1. M01

    Propellant load

    Methane, oxygen and a metered Tibanna additive. Get the mixture right or explain the engine shutdown to the range safety officer.

  2. M02

    Autonomous landing

    A Falcon 9 holds at two kilometres above a pitching drone ship on Kamino. Let the landing droid finish, or take the stick yourself.

  3. M03

    Beskar shields

    A debris field between you and the jump point. Plate the whole hull, layer the leading edges, or fly fast and hope.

Engineering notes

SpaceX, with a Star Wars twist

Every mission is a real aerospace trade-off wearing a Rebellion paint job.

Methalox, not magic

Falcon 9 flies on RP-1 and liquid oxygen; Starship moves to subcooled methane and oxygen. Mission 01 borrows that choice, then adds a gas from Bespin.

The hoverslam

A Falcon booster cannot hover. It lights one engine and meets the deck at zero velocity. Mission 02 hands that exact profile to a landing droid over Kamino.

Shields are mass

Dragon uses PICA-X, Starship uses tiles. Mission 03 asks the same question with Beskar: cover everything, cover the leading edges, or carry nothing.

Sponsor integrations

Simulated on purpose

Auth0

The login screen is a timed, local animation with a token generated by the app itself. No identity provider is contacted and no credentials are stored.

Tiger Data

The final save writes a few million simulated coordinate rows to a local counter. The hypertable, the progress bar and the row count are presentation only.

Security

The desktop app imports only the Python standard library, opens no sockets, starts no subprocesses and keeps nothing after the window closes. The full source is in this repository.
